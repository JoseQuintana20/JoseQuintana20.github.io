Research

Integrated Visual Perception and Adaptive Control for Autonomous Quadcopter Navigation

Master's thesis completed in 2026 at the Universidad Tecnológica de Pereira.

Advisors: Eduardo Giraldo-Suárez and Sergio Velarde-Gómez

Research Overview

The research proposes an integrated framework for quadcopter navigation in dynamic three-dimensional environments under computational and actuation constraints. It combines dynamic modeling, optimal control with integral action and saturation handling, supervised online adaptation, voxel-based occupancy maps, global and local planning, trajectory smoothing and embedded-platform trajectory tracking.
